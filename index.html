The home

Freshly renovated

Unit 118 has been renovated with a new kitchen built around a stone island, new floors, and all-new furnishings in soft whites, natural rattan and warm wood. Both bedrooms have king beds and room-darkening curtains, and there's a queen sofa bed in the living room.

It sits on the ground floor, so the living room and main bedroom open straight onto a private patio. From there it's a short walk past the pool to the sand.

The resort

Everything a resort offers, none of the crowds

01

The cove

A curved rock breakwater shelters the beach, so the water inside stays calm and clear. It's ideal for an easy swim, snorkelling or floating with the kids.

02

The pool

The largest pool on Seven Mile Beach, with a waterfall, hot tub, children's pool and a thatched swim-up bar. The Lazy Lizard serves lunch and dinner on site.

03

The beach

A garden path leads from the pool deck straight onto Seven Mile Beach, with loungers and umbrellas waiting on the sand.

The location

The quiet end of Seven Mile Beach

Sunset Cove sits at the calm southern end of the beach. Restaurants and bars are a short walk away, Camana Bay is fifteen minutes on foot, and the airport is about ten minutes by car.
